
## The pillars of DevOps

**five areas**

### 1. reducing toil

- Toil consists of manual, repetitive, tactical, and automatable tasks that lack long-term value, such as manual server reboots, patching, or routine ticketing.
- Goal: Free up engineers from "grunt work" to focus on higher-value engineering tasks.
- DevOps Approach: Identify toilsome tasks, measure the time spent on them, and automate or eliminate them.
